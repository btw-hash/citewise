# Security Handbook

The minimum every Larkspur Freight employee needs to know about security.

## Passwords and sign-in

All company accounts are accessed through single sign-on with a hardware security key. Passwords live in the company password manager and are never shared in chat or email. Shared mailboxes are accessed through delegation, not a shared password.

## Laptops

Laptops are encrypted by IT before they are handed over. The screen locks automatically after five minutes of inactivity. Operating system updates must be installed within seven days of release.

## Reporting an incident

Report a lost device, a suspicious email or a suspected data leak to the security channel within one hour of noticing it. Do not investigate on your own and do not delete the suspicious message. The security team answers around the clock and nobody is penalised for reporting a false alarm.

## Customer data

Customer shipment data may only be stored in systems on the approved list. Exporting customer data to a personal device or a personal cloud account is prohibited. Access to production data is granted for 30 days and must be requested again after that.

## Visitors

Visitors sign in at reception and wear a badge at all times. A visitor is never left alone in areas where shipment manifests are displayed.
