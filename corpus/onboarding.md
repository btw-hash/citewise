# Onboarding Guide

What happens in your first weeks at Larkspur Freight.

## First day

Your laptop and security key are handed over at 09:30 on your first day, in person or by courier for remote employees. You meet your onboarding buddy the same morning. The first day has no meetings after 15:00 so you can finish account setup.

## Probation period

The probation period lasts three months. Your manager holds a check-in after 30 days and a formal review after 80 days. During probation the notice period is two weeks for both sides.

## Onboarding buddy

Every new employee is paired with a buddy from a different team for the first six weeks. The buddy is not your manager and does not take part in your review. Buddies receive one extra day of leave for each person they onboard.

## Required training

Security training must be completed within the first five working days. Anti-bribery training is due within 30 days. Access to customer systems is not granted until security training is complete.

## Equipment

The standard kit is a laptop, a security key and a headset. A second monitor can be ordered from IT after the first month. Equipment is returned within ten days of the last working day.
