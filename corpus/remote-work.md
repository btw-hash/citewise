# Remote Work Policy

Larkspur Freight is a hybrid company. This policy explains where and when employees work.

## Office days

Employees based within 60 kilometres of an office come in on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Teams may agree on a third shared day, but it cannot be made mandatory. Employees living farther than 60 kilometres are classed as fully remote and visit the office once per quarter for planning week.

## Core hours

Everyone is expected to be reachable between 10:00 and 15:00 Central European Time. Outside core hours you arrange your own schedule. Meetings should not be booked before 09:00 or after 17:30 without the consent of every attendee.

## Working from abroad

You may work from another country for up to 30 calendar days per year. Stays longer than 14 consecutive days need written approval from your manager and the People team at least three weeks in advance. Working from countries under trade sanctions is not permitted under any circumstances.

## Home office allowance

New employees receive a one-time home office allowance of 400 euros for furniture and peripherals. Receipts are submitted through the expense tool within 90 days of the start date. The allowance does not cover laptops or phones, which are provided by IT.
