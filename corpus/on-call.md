# On-Call Rotation

Rules for engineers who carry the pager at Larkspur Freight.

## Schedule

The rotation changes every Monday at 11:00. Each engineer is on call for one week and then has at least three weeks off the rotation. Swaps are arranged directly between engineers and recorded in the scheduling tool.

## Response times

A critical alert must be acknowledged within 10 minutes and a high alert within 30 minutes. If the primary engineer does not acknowledge in time, the alert escalates to the secondary and then to the engineering manager.

## Compensation

On-call engineers receive a flat 350 euros per week. Work on an incident outside office hours is additionally paid at 1.5 times the hourly rate. An engineer who was paged between midnight and 06:00 may start the next working day four hours later.

## Postmortems

Every critical incident gets a written postmortem within five working days. Postmortems are blameless and are published to the whole engineering department. Action items from a postmortem are tracked until they are closed.

## Eligibility

Engineers join the rotation after six months in the company and after shadowing one full on-call week. Nobody is required to be on call during approved leave.
