# Time Off

How leave works at Larkspur Freight.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 26 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave is requested in the HR portal at least two weeks before the first day off. Requests for more than ten consecutive working days need approval from the department head.

## Carry-over

Up to five unused leave days carry over into the next year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March. Days above the limit are forfeited and are not paid out.

## Sick leave

Tell your manager before your first meeting of the day if you are sick. A doctor's note is required from the fourth consecutive day of illness. Sick days do not reduce your annual leave balance.

## Parental leave

Larkspur Freight tops up statutory pay to full salary for the first 16 weeks of parental leave. The top-up applies to all parents, including adoptive parents, after six months of employment.

## Public holidays

Public holidays follow the calendar of the country in your employment contract. A holiday that falls on a weekend is not moved to a weekday.
