# Expense Policy

This policy covers what Larkspur Freight reimburses and how to claim it.

## Submitting a claim

Claims are submitted in the expense tool with a photo of the receipt. Submit each claim within 30 days of the purchase date. Claims submitted later than 60 days are rejected unless a director signs an exception.

## Approval limits

Your direct manager approves claims up to 500 euros. Claims between 500 and 2,500 euros also need the department head. Anything above 2,500 euros requires the finance director and must be requested before the money is spent.

## Meals and travel

The meal limit on business trips is 45 euros per day. Alcohol is never reimbursed. Train travel is booked in second class for journeys under four hours and first class for longer journeys. Flights are economy class unless the flight is longer than six hours.

## Reimbursement timing

Approved claims are paid with the next payroll run if they are approved by the 20th of the month. Claims approved after the 20th are paid one month later.

## Software and subscriptions

Software purchases under 50 euros per month can be expensed without prior approval. Any tool that stores customer data must pass a security review before purchase, regardless of price.
